# Execute: Business Report

## Objective

Prepare a structured, evidence-based business report for the business or project identified by the user.

## Procedure

1. Resolve the subject, reporting period, intended audience, and purpose from the current context. Ask only for essential missing details.
2. Identify relevant available sources: project documents, connected business systems, CRM or analytics data, financial records, prior reports, and user-provided material.
3. Use connected sources when available and authorized. Do not imply access to data that has not been retrieved.
4. Establish the reporting period, data coverage, definitions, and any known gaps before drawing conclusions.
5. Analyze performance, trends, objectives, risks, operational issues, and opportunities relevant to the subject.
6. Distinguish measured facts from estimates, assumptions, and recommendations. Show calculations and denominators where material.
7. Compare with prior periods or targets only when comparable data exists.
8. Produce a clear report with an executive summary, key metrics, findings, interpretation, risks, and prioritized recommendations.
9. If the user specifies a report template, model, destination, or storage system, inspect and follow that model. Save the report only through an available, authorized integration. Verify the resulting record or file before claiming it was stored.

## Constraints

- Never fabricate metrics, sources, customer feedback, or financial results.
- State missing data and coverage limitations.
- Do not treat correlation as causation.
- Do not present forecasts as actual results.

## Final output

Use a professional, decision-oriented format. Include the reporting period, source coverage, key findings, recommendations, and any caveats. If persistence was requested, provide the verified destination and link or identifier.
