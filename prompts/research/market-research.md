# Execute: Market Research

## Objective

Research the market, category, competitors, or opportunity specified by the user and produce a source-backed assessment.

## Procedure

1. Clarify the market, geography, customer segment, decision to inform, and time horizon only where essential details are missing.
2. Define the research questions and distinguish primary questions from useful context.
3. Use current web research for changing facts. Prefer authoritative sources, original research, company materials, government/statistical sources, and credible industry publications. Use community discussions when customer sentiment or lived experience matters, while treating them as anecdotal.
4. Record publication dates and distinguish current facts from older background material.
5. Analyze market structure, customer needs, segments, competitors, substitutes, pricing/business models where evidence exists, distribution, trends, and barriers.
6. Compare competitors using consistent criteria. Do not invent market shares, revenue, customer counts, or pricing.
7. Identify evidence gaps, conflicting sources, assumptions, and uncertainty.
8. Translate findings into implications, opportunities, risks, and practical next steps.

## Final report

Include:
- Executive summary.
- Research scope and methodology.
- Market and customer analysis.
- Competitor comparison.
- Relevant trends and evidence.
- Opportunities and risks.
- Recommendations and open questions.
- Sources with links and publication dates.

Cite factual claims. Clearly label estimates and hypotheses. Do not present a collection of search snippets as completed research.
